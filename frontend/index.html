Seven modules, one platform

Health Scan

Heart rate (rPPG), blink rate, fatigue, posture, expression and a wellness summary from your webcam.

 Factory Monitor

Full-body dotted tracking, PPE check (helmet, gloves, shoes), phone-distraction flash, workstation objects, activity state.

 Student Tracking

Attentive or distracted? Expression, gaze and phone use scored live, plus a student health read-out and report.

 Sign → Speech

Say “command mode”, then signs become natural sentences, translated into regional languages and spoken. Raise both palms to call for help.

 Advanced Health

Thermal-style body view with an estimated organ guide, and dotted-contour analysis of uploaded brain MRI scans.

 Voice Assist

Whisper-level and unclear speech decoded with personal voice patterns, then spoken back in the person’s own cloned voice.

How it works

1 · CAPTURE

The browser streams webcam frames and microphone audio to the Python backend over WebSocket / REST.

2 · ANALYSE

MediaPipe (face, pose, hands) · YOLOv8 (objects, PPE) · Whisper (speech) · MFCC voice patterns · CNN for MRI · rPPG for vitals.

3 · EXPLAIN & ACT

A RAG pipeline over your safety/health policy plus an LLM writes explanations and reports; alerts flash, log and fire a webhook.

Honest scope. A webcam cannot see inside the body: the “organ view” is an estimated anatomical guide over a pseudo-thermal image (plug in a real thermal camera for true heat). MRI analysis works on uploaded scans. Sign recognition ships with a starter vocabulary and can be extended with your own trained model. Always obtain consent for monitoring and voice cloning. Not a medical device.
