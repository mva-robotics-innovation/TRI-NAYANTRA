# PPE policy
Hard hats (helmets) are mandatory in all production areas. Safety gloves are mandatory when handling machinery, sharp parts or chemicals. Safety shoes with steel toe caps are mandatory on the shop floor. Goggles are mandatory near grinding, welding and cutting.

# Mobile phones
Personal mobile phones must not be used at workstations or near moving machinery. Using a phone at a machine is a distraction hazard; supervisors should be notified and the worker reminded politely. Repeated use is escalated.

# Emergency
If a worker signals HELP (both hands raised, open palms) or a fall is detected, the supervisor and first-aider must be alerted immediately. Stop nearby machines if safe to do so. Do not move a person who has fallen unless there is danger.

# Fatigue
Signs of fatigue include frequent long eye closures, yawning and slumped posture. Workers on machinery showing fatigue should take a 10-minute break and drink water. Persistent fatigue should be escalated to the supervisor.
