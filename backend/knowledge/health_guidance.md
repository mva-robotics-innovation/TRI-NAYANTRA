# Wellness guidance (non-diagnostic)
Resting adult heart rate is typically 60-100 beats per minute. Camera-based heart-rate estimates are approximate and affected by lighting and movement. A blink rate below 8 per minute with staring suggests screen fatigue; use the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
Shoulders tilted more than about 5 degrees or a forward-dropped head suggest poor posture; adjust chair and screen height and take stretch breaks hourly.
Students who yawn often or close their eyes for long periods may need rest, water, or a short walk. Persistent symptoms, chest pain or fainting need a doctor; this tool is not a medical device.

# Student attention
Attention drops after about 25-40 minutes of continuous focus. Short breaks, active questioning and removing phones from desks improve attention. Looking away or down for more than a few seconds suggests distraction.
